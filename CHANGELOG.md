# Changelog

## 1.0-beta

- The tracker window is resizable: drag the grip in its bottom-right corner to make it larger. The size is saved per character.
- Locking the tracker window now locks its size as well as its position.
- Added a README.

## 0.5.0

First public release.

- Tracks gold, kills and experience per hour for a session you start, stop and reset.
- Gold and XP rates are rolling: they cover the last hour, so they follow your current pace.
- Shows gross gold gained, net gold, a kill count, and the time left to your next level.
- Pet kills can count as yours (setting).
- Two window styles: minimalist and classic.
- Minimap button: left-click shows the tracker, right-click opens settings. The tooltip shows the current rates.
- Slash commands: `/tph`, `/tph start`, `/tph stop`, `/tph reset`, `/tph open`, `/tph config`.
